# Etherys — AI-enabled direct-to-consumer commerce

**Role:** Founder, product and operations lead · **Status:** Live business  
**Website:** [etherys.co.uk](https://etherys.co.uk)

## The problem

Launching and running a D2C wellness brand spans ecommerce experience, product information, visual content, organic discovery and campaign analysis. I wanted to make this manageable with a small team and an AI-enabled workflow.

## What I built and operate

| Workstream | Approach |
| --- | --- |
| Shopify storefront | Built and maintain the website using AI-assisted development. |
| Creative production | Use AI for marketing images and product-related video production. |
| Content operations | Developed a pipeline for preparing and publishing marketing content. |
| Search discovery | Apply AI to keyword research and content analysis. |
| Paid acquisition | Analyse advertising and website data to guide iterations. |

## What it demonstrates

End-to-end product ownership across **building, marketing, measuring and operating**, plus practical AI adoption inside a real business rather than standalone AI demonstrations.

**Evidence:** Visit the live [Etherys storefront](https://etherys.co.uk). Commercial reports and source code are not publicly disclosed. No unverified claims about sales, conversions or advertising ROI are made here.
