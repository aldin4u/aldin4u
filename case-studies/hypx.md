## The problem

Automating trades on a crypto derivatives platform usually means navigating trading interfaces, API keys, strategy configuration and security concerns. I wanted to make that experience more accessible without asking people to surrender control of their funds.

## What I did

I led HYPX from concept through product decisions, UX and hands-on implementation. The product combines wallet permissions, execution workflows, strategy configuration and a mobile-friendly experience. I used AI-assisted development to iterate from a working product, not a slide deck.

## Key product decisions

| Decision | Why it mattered |
| --- | --- |
| Passkey-first sign-in | Reduce account-creation friction and avoid passwords. |
| Delegated trade-only wallet permissions | Enable bot execution without giving the automation withdrawal rights. |
| ATR-adaptive DCA | Adjust entry spacing to changing market volatility rather than rely only on fixed intervals. |
| Real-time performance dashboard | Help users track positions, bots and P&L in one interface. |
| PWA and push alerts | Provide a mobile-friendly experience without separate native app distribution. |
| Fast onboarding | Design toward a first configured bot in under a minute. |

## What it demonstrates

**Zero-to-one ownership**, complex-to-simple UX, AI-assisted delivery and permission-aware product design. I integrated product discovery, experience design, technical choices and iterative release into one workflow.
